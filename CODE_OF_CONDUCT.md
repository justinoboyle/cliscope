# Code of conduct

We want a welcoming, respectful project. Be considerate, assume good intent,
discuss ideas constructively, and respect differing experiences and opinions.
Give actionable feedback and accept responsibility when a mistake causes harm.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention, and
publication of another person's private information are not acceptable. These
standards apply in project spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Maintainers will
review reports with care for confidentiality and fairness. Depending on severity
and repeated behavior, they may request a correction, remove content, or restrict
participation. Maintainers are subject to the same standards.
